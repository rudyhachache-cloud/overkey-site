THE LIBRARY

Organise

Everything the archive holds, in one place.

  • Keeps documentary collections and their parts, raw footage, B-roll, photographs, audio recordings and documents in one library
  • Keeps each item's original reference number, its details and the link to its media
  • Puts a real picture from the item's own footage on every card
  • Checks the whole library for duplicates before anything new is added
  • Shows, for each collection, how much is transcribed and how many names are confirmed
THE FILM

Watch and read

The film and its words, side by side.

  • Plays any film and moves to any moment on its timeline
  • Jumps to a passage when you click its time in the transcript
  • Shows the transcript beside the film, with a time and a speaker on every passage
  • Prepares light viewing copies in the background, so very long originals still open quickly in a browser
  • Browses a film scene by scene to find a useful shot
THE PEOPLE

Speakers

Who is talking, confirmed by a person.

  • Groups the voices in a film before anyone is named
  • Suggests names by reading the name captions on screen and the context of the conversation
  • Shows the evidence behind each suggestion
  • Lets you rename a speaker and split a passage two people share
  • Lists the passages still unresolved, for review
  • Keeps a history of every change, with undo
  • Protects two people's corrections from overwriting each other
THE SEARCH

Search and ask

From a word to the exact second.

  • Searches every transcript by word, tolerant of Arabic spelling variants
  • Takes a search by voice
  • Answers questions in plain language through an archive agent
  • Cites the exact passages behind every answer, so each can be checked
THE WORK

Research and clips

From a find to a chapter, or a clip.

  • Saves passages and organises them into chapters
  • Adds notes to any saved passage
  • Exports a research selection as a document
  • Sets in and out points on any passage and exports the clip
  • Offers framing, caption and title options for each clip

Suggestions stay suggestions until a person confirms them.

Speaker names, answers and drafts are marked as unconfirmed until someone on your team has checked them against the footage. Your originals are never altered, and the archive can run entirely on your own machines.
