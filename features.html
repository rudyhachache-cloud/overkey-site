Live television · all features

Everything the live television edition does.

The full list, in the order a programme uses it: the week before, the show itself, and the hours after. Every item is in use today.

BEFORE THE SHOW

Prep

Topics, guests, dossiers, questions and the opening text for one air date, kept as one plan the whole team edits.

  • Suggests this week's topics from news no older than 14 days, each with its sources
  • Suggests guests for each topic
  • Writes a brief for any topic you type in yourself
  • Builds a guest dossier from every past appearance, read in full
  • Pulls word-for-word quotes from those appearances, each playable at the exact second
  • Collects what the guest said elsewhere in the media over the years, newest first, each with a link
  • Lists the guest's biography, positions and key numbers, with sources
  • Finds contradictions between what the guest said then and says now, with a ready one-line question
  • Writes questions for the host, each tied to the quotes and facts it rests on
  • Writes the opening text from this week's sourced facts and shows its reading time
  • Fetches more on any part of a dossier, steered by your notes
  • Turns a name typed in Latin letters into the correct Arabic name
  • Keeps every version of the plan, with undo and restore
  • Lets several producers work on the same plan without overwriting each other
  • Sends any dossier item to the graphics as a draft card, one by one or all at once
BEFORE THE SHOW

Briefing

The printable episode file the team builds together.

  • Adds any question, quote, fact or the opening text to the file in one click
  • Refuses a duplicate and shows who added the line first
  • Groups lines by guest and topic, under titles you can rename, merge and reorder
  • Lets you edit the wording of any line in place and add notes
  • Follows a question rewritten in Prep through to its line in the file
  • Prints as a PDF or downloads as a Word document, right-to-left
  • Archives a finished file and starts a new one for the next episode
  • Opens the clip editor on any line that has an archive moment
BEFORE THE SHOW

Promises

What a guest promised on air before, and whether it happened.

  • Reads every episode the guest appeared in and extracts each promise
  • Ties each promise to its episode and second
  • Checks each one online and marks it delivered, partly delivered or not delivered
  • Keeps a running ledger per politician
  • Shows the results on the guest's dossier and as on-air cards
DURING THE SHOW

Live

The show, read as it airs.

  • Transcribes the broadcast as it happens, including spoken dialect
  • Tags who is talking with one key press, so every new line carries the name
  • Names the speakers of untagged lines, and a producer's own tag always wins
  • Extracts factual claims as they are said
  • Verifies each claim against the web and your own archive
  • Gives each claim a verdict, a short explanation and dated sources
  • Re-checks a claim with a second model on request
  • Flags opinions, positions, promises and quotable lines as they are said
  • Turns any claim or moment into a draft graphic card in one click
  • Plays the live recording with rewind: click a line to hear it again
DURING THE SHOW

Desk

The analysis a producer reads at the break.

  • Analyses any time window of the show for the guests you choose
  • Lists each question asked and whether each guest answered or deflected, with the quote
  • Reads each guest's tone
  • Picks each guest's strongest lines
  • Measures talk time, the longest uninterrupted run and who cut in on whom
  • Repeats the analysis every few minutes on its own, with no browser open
  • Compares each guest's talk share with a fair share and points out the quiet guest
  • Lists numbers still unverified and false claims that have no card yet
  • Counts how often chosen words were said, per speaker
  • Turns the analysis into full-screen cards that a producer reviews and edits before air
DURING THE SHOW

On-air graphics

Cards drawn in your channel's identity and sent to air from one control page.

  • More than 50 designs: name straps, lower thirds, side boxes and full screens
  • Card types for a fact-check verdict, a number, a statement, an archive quote and a media quote
  • A contradiction card showing then and now side by side
  • A promise card with its delivery status
  • A picture card for an uploaded image, with caption and source
  • A video card that plays a finished clip
  • Analysis cards for one guest, head to head, or a grid of several
  • Condenses text to fit each design's word budget, and can shorten, lengthen or restore it
  • Lets you pick any compatible design for a card without retyping it
  • Groups cards by guest in running order
  • Previews exactly what will air, on the same renderer
  • Freezes the screen at the moment of Take, so later edits never change what is on air
  • Warns when a card is over its word budget or rests on a weak source
  • Selects, archives or deletes many cards at once
  • Outputs a transparent overlay page for the vision mixer
  • Gives the operator a separate deck for airing cards
DURING THE SHOW

Charts

A number said on air becomes a chart.

  • Builds a chart from the figure said on air and the figures the fact-check found
  • Searches your archive for earlier times the same figure was said
  • Finds the figure's history online, every point with its outlet, link and date
  • Ten designs: columns, bars, line, scale and a single large figure
  • Full screen, lower third, top corner or a side column next to the picture
  • Every value editable before it becomes a card
  • Refuses a figure with no source, or on a weak one, until a producer confirms it
DURING THE SHOW

Polls and audience

Voting on screen and a second screen in the viewer's hand.

  • Runs live votes that viewers join from a QR code
  • Shows results on air as they come in
  • Shows each studio guest's own answer as a photo on the poll
  • Provides a results page for the studio screen
  • Asks the producer after every card airs whether to send it to viewers' phones
  • Shows aired cards on the phone with their sources, charts drawn as they aired
  • Sends finished clips to phones, which viewers can share as video
  • Takes viewer questions, with limits per person against flooding
  • Rates the questions so the producer sees the best first, and turns one into a card in a click
  • Carries a sponsor line
  • Counts opens, shares and peak viewers without identifying anyone
  • Stays off until a producer switches it on, and one switch hides everything
AFTER THE MOMENT

Clips

One studio for cutting any second of the live show or the archive.

  • Opens on the exact moment from the transcript, the dossier, the briefing or the archive
  • Exports in 16:9 or vertical 9:16
  • Keeps the speaker in frame in vertical clips
  • Fills in the captions from the transcript the moment the studio opens
  • Lets you edit captions line by line, split, merge and re-time them
  • Marks chosen words in a highlight colour
  • Burns captions in Arabic, English or French
  • Adds or removes the logo and the episode line
  • Picks a thumbnail frame where the speaker looks at the camera
  • Suggests three headlines and drafts the social caption
  • Re-transcribes a selection when the wording needs to be exact
  • Delivers the video first and the thumbnail right after
  • Keeps a library of every export to play, duplicate, rebuild or download
  • Uses the keyboard shortcuts editors already know
AFTER THE MOMENT

Social

What is worth posting, while the show is still on.

  • Proposes moments worth posting every few minutes during the show
  • Gives each one a title, the quote, the reason, a caption and key words
  • Opens the clip studio already set to that moment
  • Never repeats a moment it already proposed
  • Lets you search, dismiss and restore suggestions
ALL YEAR

Archive

Every past episode, searchable to the second.

  • Transcribes and indexes every episode
  • Searches by meaning or by exact words
  • Answers questions from the archive with quotes and timestamps
  • Filters by season, episode and date
  • Plays any result at its second
  • Counts how many times a word was said on air, by year and by episode
  • Compares up to three words and suggests related ones
  • Pairs this week's news with a word-for-word moment from the archive
  • Proposes older moments that can be posted again, scored, with a title and caption
  • Re-transcribes the full episode after the show, with speakers named
ALL YEAR

Speakers and cast

One list of people, used everywhere.

  • Keeps one catalogue of people with their other spellings
  • Suggests who is speaking in each episode, shown only after a person confirms
  • Carries confirmed names into every transcript and search result
  • Updates every past credit when a person is renamed
  • Saves tonight's cast per episode, in an order you set by dragging
ALL YEAR

Sources

Where every fact comes from, and how much to trust it.

  • Ranks news outlets on a credibility list your newsroom writes and edits
  • Shows every source with its rank, outlet, link and date
  • Prefers the strongest sources in every search it runs
  • Flags a number that rests only on a weak source as not ready for air
  • Puts official documents above any media report
  • Keeps a history of the list, with restore
ALL YEAR

Team and control

Who can do what, and who did what.

  • Gives each person an account with no access, read, or write for every part of the system
  • Adds a separate guard on anything that can reach the screen
  • Logs who did what, kept for 90 days
  • Shows who is online now
  • Keeps long tasks running after the browser closes, with a step log and a Stop button
  • Lets admins edit the editorial instructions the system works under
  • Lets each user hide and reorder the menu
  • Light and dark themes
